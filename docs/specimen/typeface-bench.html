Justified text is the whole difficulty. A browser breaks one line at a time, so a locally acceptable break can force the next line loose, opening rivers of whitespace down the column. Knuth–Plass weighs the paragraph whole—some 1,024 candidate breakings at once—and the difference shows inside the first five lines of any figure-heavy passage.

Quixotic? Perhaps. But the office of a text face is to disappear, and a face that draws attention to its own fitting has failed at that office. The fifteen flagstaff sufficiently proves the point: if you noticed the ligatures there before you noticed the sentence was nonsense, the face is working.
