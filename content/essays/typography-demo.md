## Quote Handling

"Smart quotes are automatically converted," she said. 'Even single quotes work properly.' The apostrophe in words like don't, can't, and it's also gets the curly treatment.

Notice how nested quotes work: "She said, 'Hello there,' and I replied."

## Dashes and Ellipses

The en dash is used for ranges: 2020--2024, pages 10--15, Monday--Friday.

The em dash---which is longer---is used for emphasis or parenthetical statements.

Ellipses... are also handled intelligently when you type three periods.

## Common Patterns

Here are some common typographic improvements:

- "Hello" becomes "Hello" (curly double quotes)
- 'Hello' becomes 'Hello' (curly single quotes)  
- It's becomes It's (curly apostrophe)
- 1990's becomes 1990's
- -- becomes – (en dash)
- --- becomes — (em dash)
- ... becomes … (ellipsis)

## Mathematical and Technical Context

Smart punctuation is context-aware. In code blocks, everything remains literal:

```python
print("This won't have curly quotes")
x = 5 -- 3  # Double hyphen stays as-is
```

But in regular text, you'll see the transformations: "This text--written in 2024--demonstrates the feature."

## Foreign Language Support

The system handles various quote styles. In French: « Bonjour », and in German: „Hallo". Though primarily designed for English, it respects different typographic traditions.

## Why This Matters

Good typography isn't just aesthetic---it's functional. Proper dashes clarify meaning, smart quotes improve readability, and consistent punctuation creates a professional appearance. As Robert Bringhurst wrote in "The Elements of Typographic Style": "Typography exists to honor content."
