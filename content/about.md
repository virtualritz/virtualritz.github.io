Welcome to my personal website. This is a space where I write about technology, share projects, and explore ideas that interest me.

## The Site

This website is built with [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/), a fast static site generator written in Rust. The typography and design are inspired by [gwern.net](https://gwern.net), emphasizing readability and content over decoration.

### Design Principles

- **Readability first**: Clear typography with optimal line length and spacing
- **Fast loading**: No unnecessary JavaScript, minimal dependencies
- **Accessible**: Semantic HTML, proper heading structure, sufficient contrast
- **Responsive**: Works well on all screen sizes
